— 02

The method.

Step 01

Define demand

Load your demand geography — population data, customer locations, operational catchments — mapped to Hong Kong's 18 administrative districts and weighted by density.

Step 02

Model the real network

Distances are computed on Hong Kong's actual road network via OSM — not straight lines. Travel paths follow real routes, tunnels, waterway crossings, and road classifications.

Step 03

Find optimal sites

The algorithm identifies the exact locations that minimise average travel distance to demand — maximising coverage with your given facility count via k-medoids on road distances.

— 03

Capabilities.

Coverage optimisation

Given N facilities to place, find the configuration that maximises demand within a specified travel-distance threshold. Solved via k-medoids on a real road-distance matrix — not Euclidean approximation.

Network gap analysis

Identify underserved zones in your existing facility network. Quantify where demand exceeds coverage thresholds and surface the service deficit by district and sub-region.

Road-aware routing

All distance calculations traverse Hong Kong's OSM-derived road network. One-way streets, tunnels, harbour crossings, and elevation-adjusted route preferences are fully accounted for.

Demand modelling

7.5M-resident dataset disaggregated across HK's 18 districts, with weighting for density, commercial land use, and operator-specific demand profiles for logistics, EV charging, or field service networks.
